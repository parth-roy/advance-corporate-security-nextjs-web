## 4. Statutory Cost Sheet & Wage Architecture

To rank #1 for enterprise procurement queries and convert CFOs/Procurement Directors, ACS embeds the transparent statutory cost formula:

### 4.1 The 1/6th Reliever Mathematical Roster
Continuous 24×7 guarding requires mandatory weekly rest under Section 13 of the *Minimum Wages Act, 1948* and Section 52 of the *Factories Act, 1948*:

$$\text{Relievers Required} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Base Guards}}{6} \right\rceil$$
$$\text{Total Billable Workforce} = \text{Base Guards} + \text{Relievers} \quad (+16.67\% \text{ Headcount})$$

### 4.2 Comprehensive Monthly Statutory Cost Structure (West Bengal Industrial Zone A)
* **Basic + VDA:** Zone A minimum wages (adjusted semi-annually per WB Labour Commissionerate).
* **Statutory Reliever Allowance:** $\frac{1}{6}$th (+16.67%) of Basic + VDA.
* **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF - Employer 13.00%):** 3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS + 0.5% EDLI + 0.5% Admin charges.
* **Employees' State Insurance (ESIC - Employer 3.25%):** Applied to gross wages up to ₹21,000.
* **Statutory Bonus (8.33%):** Computed under the *Payment of Bonus Act, 1965*.
* **Uniform & Gear Allowance:** Laundering, boots, baton, flashlight, and tactical whistle.
* **Liquid Payroll Float Guarantee:** Committed 60-day liquid reserve float ensuring workers receive wages strictly by the 7th of every month regardless of client payment terms.

3. **Scroll Signal Removal:** Suppress browser scrollbar indicators (`scrollbar-width: none; -ms-overflow-style: none; &::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none; }`).

### 5.2 FAQ Accordion Button UI Disruption Fix
1. **Root Cause:** In `advance-corporate-security-nextjs-web/src/components/`, the FAQ toggle buttons suffer from flexbox flex-shrink distortion when the accordion expands.
2. **Resolution:** Enforce `flex-shrink: 0`, explicit width/height (`w-8 h-8`), `overflow: hidden`, and proper transitions on the chevron icon across all FAQ components.

### 5.3 Desktop Horizontal Scrollbar & Overflow Fix
1. **Root Cause:** Full-bleed containers (`w-screen` or `100vw` combined with scrollbar gutters) causing horizontal scrollbar flicker.
2. **Resolution:** Replace `w-screen` with `w-full` and enforce `overflow-x: hidden` on root layout containers.
