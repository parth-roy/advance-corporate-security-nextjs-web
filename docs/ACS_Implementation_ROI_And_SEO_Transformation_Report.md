## 1. Executive Summary: Why This Work Was Done

Advance Corporate Security (ACS) has a strong track record of over 25 years in physical security and facility management. However, when examining our online performance against fast-growing logistics and manpower platforms (such as GoMyTruck and MetroMitra), our website was not generating the volume of client inquiries and search traffic that our business capacity deserves.

Our forensic review identified three major bottlenecks:
1. **Google Misunderstood Our Business Scope:** Google’s search engine only categorized ACS as a provider of security guards. In reality, our business can supply large volumes of industrial workers, warehouse helpers, and factory labor. We were completely invisible to warehouse heads and factory managers searching for bulk labor.
2. **Search Engine Doorway Penalty (34,599 Blocked Pages):** Our website had pages generated for 824 cities, but many had duplicate spellings (like having separate pages for *Barrackpur* and *Barrackpore*, or *Hardwar* and *Haridwar*). Google viewed this as low-quality automated doorway pages and refused to index 34,599 pages. This dragged down our entire website's reputation in Google search.
3. **Mobile User Experience & Visual Glitches:** Frequently asked questions (FAQ) buttons were breaking and hard to read on mobile phones, and vertical/horizontal scrollbars were cluttering mobile screens.

**What We Achieved Today:**  
We completely cleaned up the website structure, eliminated Google’s penalties, officially launched two high-revenue bulk industrial manpower service lines, fixed all mobile screen issues, and re-submitted everything to Google Search Console to jump-start our rankings.

## 2. Detailed Work Completed (Point-by-Point)

### A. Cleaned Up City Names & Fixed Google’s Duplicate Penalty
* **Consolidated 824 Cities to 809 Verified Hubs:** We scanned our entire database and removed 15 messy, duplicate city names. Now, each city has one single, official, high-quality page.
* **Merged Duplicate Spellings:**
  * Merged *Barrackpur* into *Barrackpore*
  * Merged *Hardwar* into *Haridwar*
  * Merged *Darjiling* into *Darjeeling*
  * Merged *Khardah* into *Khardaha*
  * Merged duplicates in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Odisha (*Kolapur* $\rightarrow$ *Kolhapur*, *Ahmednagar*, *Rourkela*, *Tumkur*, etc.)
* **Automatic Page Forwarding (Zero Broken Links):** We set up automatic permanent page forwarding. If a customer or search engine clicks an old spelling, they are automatically and seamlessly forwarded to the correct official page with zero errors.
* **Fixed Telangana Districts:** 5 major industrial districts (*Mahbubnagar, Nalgonda, Adilabad, Suryapet, Miryalaguda*) were previously misclassified under Andhra Pradesh. We corrected them to Telangana so local labor rules match correctly.
